# User Story - US_017

## Story ID
* ID Format: US_017

## Story Title
* Manual intake form with multi-section validation and save

## Description
* As a **patient**, I want to complete my pre-visit intake using a structured multi-section form as a full alternative to the AI chat, so that I can provide my health information at my own pace without AI dependency.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-001: Manual form renders all 5 required sections
- **Given**: A patient navigates to the manual intake form (SCR-005)
- **When**: The page loads
- **Then**: Five tab sections are visible: Demographics, Medical History, Medications, Allergies, and Chief Complaint — each accessible by tab click without a page reload

### AC-002: Form saves an encrypted IntakeRecord with status Complete on valid submission
- **Given**: All mandatory fields across all 5 sections are filled
- **When**: `POST /intake/manual` is called with the complete form data
- **Then**: The API returns HTTP 201, an `IntakeRecord` with `status = "Complete"` and `mode = "Manual"` is saved with encrypted JSONB data, and an audit log entry `ActionType: IntakeCompleted` is written

### AC-003: Missing mandatory field prevents submission and highlights the field
- **Given**: The Chief Complaint section has no text entered
- **When**: The patient clicks "Submit"
- **Then**: Submission is blocked, the "Chief Complaint" tab label shows a red indicator, and the error message "Chief complaint is required" appears in that section without clearing other entered data

### AC-004: Partial form data is auto-saved as a draft on navigation-away
- **Given**: A patient has filled 3 of the 5 sections and attempts to navigate away from SCR-005
- **When**: A navigation event is detected (browser back, route change)
- **Then**: `POST /intake/draft` is called automatically, an `IntakeRecord` with `status = "Draft"` is saved with the partial encrypted data, and a toast "Draft saved" appears

### AC-005: Previously started draft is pre-populated when the form is reopened
- **Given**: A patient has a Draft `IntakeRecord` for the current visit cycle
- **When**: The patient navigates to `/intake` (SCR-005)
- **Then**: The form fields are pre-populated with the draft data and a banner "Resuming your saved draft" is displayed at the top of the form

## Edge Cases
* **Invalid date format in Medical History**: If the patient enters an appointment date in a format other than `YYYY-MM-DD` or `MM/DD/YYYY`, the field must display "Please enter a valid date" and prevent submission without clearing other fields.
* **Medication entry with only brand name**: If a medication is entered with only a trade name (e.g., "Tylenol") and no dosage, the field must be accepted (not blocked) but flagged with a soft warning "Consider adding dosage for clarity" — submission must not be blocked.
