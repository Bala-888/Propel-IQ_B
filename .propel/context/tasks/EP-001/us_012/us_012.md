# User Story - US_012

## Story ID
* ID Format: US_012

## Story Title
* Staff-assisted walk-in patient account creation

## Description
* As a **staff member**, I want to optionally create a patient account when processing a walk-in booking, so that the walk-in patient can access their visit history and future bookings online without re-registering.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-001: Walk-in booking proceeds without account creation
- **Given**: A staff member is on the Walk-in Booking Form (SCR-012) with the "Create account" toggle off
- **When**: The walk-in booking is submitted
- **Then**: The booking is created without a linked patient account and the patient appears in the queue (SCR-011) with status "Walk-in — No account"

### AC-002: Account creation modal captures email and temporary password
- **Given**: The "Create account" toggle is enabled in the Walk-in Booking Form
- **When**: The staff member submits the form with a valid patient email
- **Then**: MOD-004 (Walk-in Account Creation) captures the email; the system creates a Patient account with a system-generated temporary password and links the booking to the new patient ID

### AC-003: New account credentials emailed to the patient
- **Given**: Account creation succeeds for the walk-in patient
- **When**: `POST /walkins` completes with `createAccount: true`
- **Then**: An email is dispatched to the patient's provided address with subject "Your account has been created" containing the temporary password and a link to the login page within 30 seconds

### AC-004: Duplicate email for walk-in account creation is handled gracefully
- **Given**: A patient account already exists with `email = "john.doe@email.com"`
- **When**: Staff enters the same email in MOD-004 and submits
- **Then**: The API returns HTTP 409 and the modal displays "An account with this email already exists. Would you like to link this walk-in to that account?" with Yes/No options

## Edge Cases
* **Staff skips the email field in MOD-004**: If the "Create account" toggle is on but the email field is empty, the form must prevent submission and display "Email is required to create an account".
* **Email delivery failure**: If the credentials email fails all 3 retries, the walk-in booking must still succeed and the failure must be logged in Seq; the staff interface must show a warning "Credentials email could not be delivered — please provide credentials manually".
