# User Story - US_027

## Story ID
* ID Format: US_027

## Story Title
* Send automated appointment reminder via email and SMS before scheduled visit

## Description
* As a **patient**, I want to receive an automated reminder email and SMS before my appointment, so that I am not caught off-guard and can reschedule if needed.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-001: Reminder is dispatched 24 hours before the appointment time
- **Given**: A patient has a Confirmed booking with `appointment_datetime` 24 hours from now
- **When**: The scheduled reminder job runs
- **Then**: The patient receives an email with subject `"Reminder: Your appointment is tomorrow – <Date>"` and an SMS `"Reminder: UPACIP appointment on <Date> at <Time>. Reply STOP to opt out."` — both dispatched within a 5-minute window of the 24-hour threshold

### AC-002: A second reminder is dispatched 2 hours before the appointment time
- **Given**: The 24-hour reminder has already been sent and the booking is still Confirmed
- **When**: The reminder job runs at the 2-hour threshold
- **Then**: A second email and SMS are sent with subject/body updated to `"Your appointment is in 2 hours"` — no duplicate of the 24-hour reminder is sent

### AC-003: Reminder is not sent if the booking has been cancelled
- **Given**: A booking was cancelled after the 24-hour reminder was sent
- **When**: The 2-hour reminder job runs
- **Then**: No notification is dispatched and the Seq log records `eventType = "ReminderSkipped"` with `reason = "BookingCancelled"`

### AC-004: Reminder respects patient notification preferences
- **Given**: A patient has `sms_notifications_enabled = false`
- **When**: The reminder job processes the booking
- **Then**: Only the email reminder is sent — no SMS is dispatched

## Edge Cases
* **Booking created less than 24 hours before the appointment**: If a booking is created with fewer than 24 hours remaining, the 24-hour reminder is skipped and only the 2-hour reminder is scheduled — the 24-hour reminder must not fire retrospectively.
* **Appointment time changes (reschedule)**: When a booking's slot is rescheduled, all previously scheduled reminder jobs for that booking must be cancelled and new ones registered from the updated `appointment_datetime`.
