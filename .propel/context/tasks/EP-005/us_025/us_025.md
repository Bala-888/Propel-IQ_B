# User Story - US_025

## Story ID
* ID Format: US_025

## Story Title
* Background job automatically swaps patient to preferred slot when it becomes available

## Description
* As a **clinic system**, I want a background job to detect when a preferred slot becomes available and automatically move the matching patient's booking to that slot, so that patient preferences are honoured without requiring manual intervention.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-001: Preferred slot monitor job runs on a configurable schedule (default: every 5 minutes)
- **Given**: The background job is registered in the .NET 8 hosted service pipeline
- **When**: 5 minutes elapse since the last execution
- **Then**: `SELECT * FROM preferred_slots JOIN bookings ON ... WHERE preferred_slot_status = 'Available'` is executed and all eligible swap candidates are processed

### AC-002: Eligible patient booking is atomically swapped to the preferred slot
- **Given**: A slot that was previously Booked (by a cancellation) now has `status = "Available"` and at least one patient has it registered as their preferred slot
- **When**: The background job processes the eligible candidate
- **Then**: In a single ACID transaction: the original booking is updated to `status = "Cancelled"`, a new booking is created for the preferred slot with `status = "Confirmed"`, the preferred slot row is updated to `status = "Booked"`, and the original slot is updated to `status = "Available"` — all four writes succeed or all four roll back

### AC-003: When multiple patients prefer the same slot, the earliest registration wins
- **Given**: Patients A and B both have the same preferred slot registered, with Patient A registering first
- **When**: That slot becomes available
- **Then**: Patient A's booking is swapped and Patient B's preferred slot designation remains (for the next opportunity or manual resolution) — `SELECT COUNT(*) FROM bookings WHERE slot_id = <slotId> AND status = 'Confirmed'` returns 1

### AC-004: Swap failure does not corrupt either the original or target booking
- **Given**: The preferred slot swap transaction encounters a constraint error mid-way
- **When**: The transaction rolls back
- **Then**: Both the patient's original booking and the preferred slot record remain in their pre-swap state — neither booking is left in a `null` or `Cancelled` state without a replacement

### AC-005: Completed swap triggers a `SlotSwapCompleted` domain event
- **Given**: An ACID swap transaction commits successfully
- **When**: The hosted service completes the swap
- **Then**: A `SlotSwapCompleted` domain event is raised with `patientId`, `oldSlotId`, and `newSlotId` — consumed by us_026 for notification dispatch

## Edge Cases
* **Preferred slot becomes blocked (not just booked) by an admin before the job runs**: If the slot status is `Blocked` at job execution time, the patient's preferred slot designation must be preserved for re-evaluation in the next job cycle — it must not be removed automatically.
* **High-volume: 500 eligible candidates in a single job run**: The job must process candidates in batches of 50 using a configurable `BatchSize` setting to avoid long-held transactions and lock escalation.
